# Radik Stage05 — experience that changes the next check

**Stage05 is accepted within its original qualified-experience profile: 10 of 10 requirements.**

What should an assistant do after a check exposes an unresolved condition? Radik's Stage05 records a bounded answer: retain a qualified observation, reconsider which check is useful next, and keep that lesson tied to the task and sources that produced it.

This is a guided reading of completed executions. The documents are fictional and already in English; the model calls and native Work are real. No demonstration was rerun to produce this page.

## 1. Start with a support question

The owner asks: *Explain the support conditions of fictional Service Rowan, preserving eligibility, exceptions and unknown charges.* Rowan supports active annual plans, promises a reply to urgent requests within one working day, excludes telephone support on public holidays and leaves call prices unspecified. A normal Orchestrator/Architect/Critic round produces an accepted answer.

The experience mechanism then records an expectation before making its native observation. The eligible Boolean result is zero. This observation concerns an approved raw-source predicate; it does not label the ordinary answer a failure or grade the model's general intelligence.

## 2. Watch the next check change

Before the observation, the current consumer chooses `source_verification`. After qualified history is admitted under fresh checks, it chooses `source_condition_audit`. The result is another actual PURE Work output, containing a concrete unresolved-source finding. The index connects expectation, outcome, history, decision and consumed result; the change is observable in the work, not just in a comment about learning.

## 3. Change the subject

The next task concerns Museum Alder: opening hours, a garden closed during thunderstorms, a one-visit ticket and separately booked tours. Its own accepted result has a different task, Root and source scope. It gets no Rowan history, prior or prohibition and performs its own cold verification. Rowan's stored history stays unchanged. This is scoped-key isolation; it does not establish that Rowan's now-aged history would still qualify as CURRENT under a hypothetical same-scope lookup.

## 4. Change the original document

Rowan's document receives a new revision and fictional call price. The old live binding is refused with `dynamic_current_sources_changed` before dispatch. Existing Work and history remain intact. The source-first refusal occurs before TTL and Work expiry but after the separate 300-second maximum-age interval; continued CURRENT freshness at that point is not claimed.

## 5. Account for the work already accepted

Earlier independently accepted G4 evidence supplies the allocation part of the composite examination. Seven checks shift from four quotation / three qualification to three quotation / four qualification. Both runs spend the same nine units, including bootstrap and final consumption. The final Work consumes all seven selected outputs. A hard exclusion still prevents a forbidden branch from running despite favorable score inputs. This is redistribution, not acceleration.
